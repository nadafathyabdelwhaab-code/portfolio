About

Words first, then numbers.

I started as a content creator in 2020, writing email campaigns, SMS messages and lead-generation content for clients in the Gulf and Egypt. That work taught me what makes people reply, book and come back.

For the last two years I've moved into media planning and paid ads. I build the plan, set up the campaigns on TikTok, Meta and Snapchat, write the acquisition content, and read the numbers every week to decide what to cut and what to scale.
